# SmolLM2: When Smol Goes Big — Data-Centric Training of a Small Language Model  (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02737, 2025, Ben Allal, Lozhkov, Bakouch, ... von Werra, Wolf; Hugging Face)

Read in full: arXiv HTML v1 (all sections, Tables 1-11). Also read: SmolLM2-135M/360M model cards and configs, smol-smoltalk and everyday-conversations dataset cards. Tags: [P] paper, [HF] Hugging Face card/config, [I] my inference.

## What it is
A data-engineering report for a 1.7B Llama-2-style model trained on ~11T tokens, with 360M and 135M siblings. Its contribution is the data: ablated web mixes, new filtered math/code sets, and SmolTalk, a 1.1M-example synthetic instruction/chat set. For us it is the best-documented recipe for chat-tuning sub-200M models, and SmolLM2-135M is a direct size-peer of GPT-2 small.

## Mechanism
**Pretraining data selection [P, Sec. 3-4].** Ablate each candidate source by training on it, then mix by benchmark. FineWeb-Edu (educational-quality classifier) is better for knowledge; DCLM (more conversational/forum-like web) is better for commonsense. 1.7B: four manual stages, re-weighting after each.
**Small models [P, Sec. 6].** Single stage, fixed mix. "Filtering DCLM with the FineWeb-Edu classifier, removing samples with score 0, and downsampling those with scores 1 and 2 worked best"; Stack-Edu, InfiMM-WebMath, FineMath, Cosmopedia from the start. Mix ratios are not given.
**Architecture [HF configs].** 135M: 30 layers, width 576, FFN 1536, 9 heads / 3 KV heads (GQA), RoPE, tied embeddings, vocab 49,152. 360M: 32 layers, width 960, FFN 2560, 15/5 heads.
**SmolTalk [P, Sec. 5.1, Table 9].**
- MagPie-Ultra (431k kept of 1M): prompt the teacher with only the pre-query chat template plus a system prompt so it writes the user turn itself, then answer; three turns. Teacher Llama-3.1-405B. Filter with Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-Guard; score with ArmoRM; dedup by gte-large embeddings.
- Smol-Constraints 36.2k (550k generated by Qwen2.5-72B, 56.3k after removing conflicting constraints/wrong answers, 36k after 10-gram decontamination against IFEval).
- Smol-Summarization 101k, Smol-Rewrite 56.2k (Qwen2.5-72B): persona-conditioned source texts (emails, tweets, notes), then summarize/rewrite.
- Existing sets fill the rest: math 162k, OpenHermes2.5 100k, function calling 87.5k, code 50.7k, system prompts 35.9k, Everyday-Conversations 2.38k, others.
**smol-smoltalk [HF].** 460,341 train / 24,229 test. Versus SmolTalk: shorter MagPie conversations, no function calling, fewer rewrite/summarize examples, no advanced math, "since these smaller models have limited capacity".
**Everyday-Conversations [HF].** 2.2k chats from Llama-3.1-70B: 20 everyday topics x 100 subtopics + 43 elementary-science topics x 10; 3-4 short exchanges; fixed greeting opener, randomised across four forms.

- Table 10 [P] (1.7B base, 1 epoch SFT), IFEval / MT-Bench: OpenHermes 30.01 / 1.02; UltraChat 27.26 / 4.66; MagPie-Pro 30.45 / 4.31; MagPie-Pro multi-turn 31.66 / 5.40; MagPie-Ultra 35.49 / 5.22; + three Smol sets 48.16 / 5.28; full SmolTalk 46.67 / 5.49; 2 epochs 57.09 / 6.11.
- 135M base [HF]: HellaSwag 42.1, ARC avg 43.9, PIQA 68.4, MMLU cloze 31.5, CommonsenseQA 33.9, TriviaQA 4.1, GSM8K 1.4. Against SmolLM v1 135M: 41.2, 42.4, 68.4, 30.2.
- 135M-Instruct [HF]: IFEval 29.9 (v1: 17.2), HellaSwag 40.9, ARC 37.3; MT-Bench printed as "19.8" (v1 "16.8"), a different scale from the 360M card.
- 360M [HF]: HellaSwag 54.5, ARC 53.0, TriviaQA 16.9; Instruct IFEval 41.0, MT-Bench 3.66.
- Math filtering [P]: Infi-WebMath4+ plateaus after ~80B tokens (~10 epochs), "likely due to data repetition".

## Smallest scale tested, and whether the result held there
135M, trained on 2T tokens. All ablation tables are at 1.7B; the paper says ablations were re-run for 135M/360M but prints none. What held, by assertion: quality-filtered web plus trimmed chat data. What did not transfer: multi-stage curricula (single stage was better) and the full SmolTalk (had to be simplified). Nothing below 135M, and 135M is 100x our dense-compute budget.

## Training recipe and gotchas
- 135M/360M: AdamW betas (0.9, 0.95), 2,000-step warmup, hold at LR 3e-3, decay over the last 20%; 64 H100s [HF]. (1.7B: LR 5e-4, 10% decay.)
- SFT: 2 epochs, batch 128, LR 3e-4. DPO on UltraFeedback: 2 epochs, LR 1e-6, beta 0.5, sequence 1,024.
- Epoch 2 of SFT mattered: IFEval 46.67 to 57.09 (Table 10).
- OpenHermes alone gives MT-Bench 1.02: single-turn instruction data does not produce a multi-turn chatbot.
- Everyday-Conversations card: without it, models "failed to answer basic prompts such as 'Hi'" and "Who are you".
- 2T tokens is ~10,000x what one 5090 can push through; only the data design transfers [I].

## Mapping onto the GBA design
Zero run-time cost: this changes training data only. Two things map.
1. Chat data: build "koko-talk" as smol-smoltalk filtered further (short turns, simple vocabulary) plus a scaled-up Everyday-Conversations (same topic x subtopic grid, generated by our open-weight teacher), plus identity/greeting dialogues. Use the MagPie self-instruct trick so user turns are also model-written; that gives realistic user-side text for predictive typing.
2. Pretraining text: score candidate text with a quality classifier, drop the bottom class, downsample the next two, single stage.
SmolLM2-135M/360M are also candidate distillation teachers (logits are cheap on a 5090) and a second baseline next to GPT-2 [I].

## Verdict
**Adopt** the data recipe (trimmed chat mix, everyday-conversation grid, classifier-filtered single-stage pretraining, 2 SFT epochs); **skip** DPO, math, code, function calling. Settling experiment: fine-tune one fixed tiny base on (a) raw smol-smoltalk, (b) short-turn filtered subset + 50k generated everyday chats; compare held-out chat perplexity and judge-graded replies to "Hi", "Who are you", and 3-turn follow-ups.
