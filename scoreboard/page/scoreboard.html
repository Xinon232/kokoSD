Koko against five public small models

Koko is a chatbot that runs on an unmodified Game Boy Advance from a 32 MB cartridge. This page measures it against small public language models on the same text, the same questions and the same prompts. Measured 20 November 2026.

The short version

  • Chat: blind judges preferred Koko's replies to SmolLM2-135M-Instruct's on 18 of 20 everyday messages, and to GPT-2's on all 20.
  • Stories: judges preferred Koko to GPT-2 (19 of 20) and to both TinyStories models (15 and 16 of 20), and rated it about level with SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (9 to 11).
  • Facts: Koko alone is weak. It answered 22 of 60 general-knowledge questions; SmolLM2-135M answered 43. It knows capitals (35 of 40) because it was drilled on them.
  • Size: Koko is the only model here built to fit a cartridge. The others are 15 to 540 MB as published.
  • Cost: Koko was trained in 7.4 hours on one rented graphics card.

The judges were nine separate runs of an AI model (Claude), not people, and the same family of model wrote Koko's code. The messages are the kind Koko was trained for. The TinyStories models and GPT-2 do not follow instructions, so they often ignored the subject; that is a real difference in ability, but it means this compares "asked for a story about X", not raw story-writing.

  • The Game Boy Advance figures were measured in the mGBA emulator, not on a real console.
  • A Game Boy Advance runs for roughly 15 hours on two AA batteries, which is about half a watt. At 3 tokens per second that is on the order of 0.2 joules per token. This is an estimate from battery life, not a measurement.

How to read this

  • One run of each test, with fixed random seeds. Small differences are not meaningful.
  • Koko on the cartridge samples at temperature 0.5 and blocks repeated phrases; the other models sampled at 0.7 for stories and chat. Question answering used each model's most likely answer.
  • Sources for the other models' training: the SmolLM2-135M model card, the TinyStories paper, and OpenAI's GPT-2 announcement with its model card.
