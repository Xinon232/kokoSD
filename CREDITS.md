## What the model was trained on
- **TinyStories** (Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li), CDLA-Sharing-1.0. https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories
- **SODA** (Allen Institute for AI), CC BY 4.0. https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/soda
- **TinyDialogues** (Steven Y. Feng and others), MIT. https://huggingface.co/datasets/styfeng/TinyDialogues
- **Everyday Conversations** (Hugging Face), Apache 2.0. https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/everyday-conversations-llama3.1-2k
  The conversations in it were generated by Meta's Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct (Llama 3.1 Community License). Built with Llama.
- **Simple English Wikipedia** (snapshot of 1 November 2023 from https://huggingface.co/datasets/wikimedia/wikipedia), CC BY-SA and GFDL.
- **Country facts** from mledoze/countries (https://github.com/mledoze/countries), Open Database License; used to
  write question-and-answer drills (`gen/drills.py`).
- **Koko's own conversations:** about 1.3 million short chats written by the Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct model
  (Alibaba Cloud, Apache 2.0) from prompts written for this project (`gen/`), then filtered. They are machine-written.

None of the training data is included in this repository; the scripts in `train/` and `gen/` download or build it.

## Stage 1 (src/, tools/)
- `tools/fetch.sh` downloads the **stories260K** model and its tokenizer from Andrej Karpathy's tinyllamas
  (https://huggingface.co/karpathy/tinyllamas, MIT, from the llama2.c project https://github.com/karpathy/llama2.c)
  and the **font8x8** bitmap font by Daniel Hepper (https://github.com/dhepper/font8x8, public domain).
  `gen/font.h` is generated from font8x8. Neither download is included.

## Scoreboard (scoreboard/)
- The comparison models are downloaded from Hugging Face when the scripts run and are not included: GPT-2
  (OpenAI, MIT, https://huggingface.co/openai-community/gpt2), SmolLM2-135M and SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (Hugging Face,
  Apache 2.0), TinyStories-1M/8M/33M (Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li, https://huggingface.co/roneneldan; their model
  cards state no licence). `scoreboard/samples.json` and the judge
  transcripts in `scoreboard/judge/` contain short texts those models generated for the comparison.
- The judges were runs of an AI model (Claude), not people.
- The question sets in `scoreboard/qa_sets.py` were written by hand for this project.
- The scoreboard page loads the Inter and Space Grotesk fonts (both SIL Open Font License 1.1) from Google Fonts;
  they are not included.
